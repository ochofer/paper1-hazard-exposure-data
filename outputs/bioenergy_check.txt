  WHY THE TWO DIFFER, and it is the conventions section's own point.
  Under blank=zero, 33 of the March 2025 firms carry attributable
  capacity of exactly zero, because every ownership edge they have has a
  blank share and a blank multiplied by zero is nothing. Under blank=impute
  that count is 0. So the convention does not only change the values, it
  decides whether a firm registers as having exposure at all. Those tied
  zeros are what drag the blank=zero rank correlation down. Filtering them
  out removes the convention's effect, which is the thing being measured.

  U2 STRADDLES THE CUT. The convention flip is GENERAL, not an artefact
  of the regional panel, and the note says so. It reproduces on 604
  firms worldwide and on 171 panel firms, in the same direction.
